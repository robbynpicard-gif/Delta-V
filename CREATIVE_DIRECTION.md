# DELTA-V — Creative Direction

**Status:** Approved direction; implementation and exact assets undecided.
**Updated:** 2026-11-23.

## Visual — APPROVED
Blend atmospheric minimalism (C) with clean vector minimalism (A). Deep navy space, extremely sparse stars, geometric tiny recognizable spacecraft, elegant fine orbit lines, soft planetary gradients and restrained atmospheric glows. Smooth camera movement and generous negative space. Interface should be unobtrusive except during manoeuvres; clarity of trajectory is paramount. Avoid clutter, photorealistic demands and excessive effects.

## Audio and emotion — APPROVED
Reference the *principles* of Mini Metro: simple, restrained, hypnotic, musical sound design. Overall mood is **peaceful and meditative, occasionally tense** — NOT constantly escalating rhythm or action music. Original sounds/compositions only, never copied motifs or samples. Silence is valuable; avoid incessant clicking, alarms, engine noise, bombast and busy music.

## Sound concepts — PROPOSED, for experimentation
- Sparse synthesized ambient bed with long pauses.
- Soft tonal feedback on manoeuvre hold; subtly responsive pitch/timbre while adjusting delta-v; restrained release/engine cue.
- Musical accents for meaningful periapsis, SOI exit, orbit capture, discovery and personal record, carefully rate-limited to avoid repetitive chimes.
- Danger may introduce low pulse/dissonance, fading naturally after resolution.
- Optional generative soundscape interpreting orbital motion without influencing physics.

## Emotional arc
Peaceful coast → focused approach → short tension during consequential manoeuvre → gentle acknowledgement → peaceful coast. Tension must be earned by gameplay. A quiet orbit should itself feel satisfying.

## Before prototype authorization
Discuss and approve a concise visual/audio style specification, interaction feedback, and a tiny original sound palette. Prototype implementation is currently blocked by the owner’s preproduction gate.
